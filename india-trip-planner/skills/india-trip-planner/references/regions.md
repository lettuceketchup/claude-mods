# Regions: seasons, permits, closures, getting there

A starting checklist per region, written from general knowledge (late 2026). Every row says what to check, not what is true for the user's dates. Confirm on official sites and recent news before giving a verdict.

## National, every trip
- **Southwest monsoon**, June to September over most of India: landslides in hills, floods in cities, rough seas. **Northeast monsoon**, October to December: Tamil Nadu and coastal Andhra, with cyclone risk on the east coast.
- **North Indian fog**, December to January: trains through Delhi, UP, Bihar and Punjab often run hours late. Leave bigger buffers.
- **Heat**, April to June in the plains, 40–47 °C in Rajasthan, Gujarat, MP, UP and Delhi.
- **Rush periods**: trains and stays sell out around Diwali, Chhath (Bihar and UP trains), Durga Puja (east), Holi, summer holidays (mid-May to June), Christmas and New Year at hill stations and Goa, and long weekends. Check the festival calendar for the travel dates.
- **Advisories**: check state government or district alerts and recent news for unrest, curfews, road closures or internet shutdowns.
