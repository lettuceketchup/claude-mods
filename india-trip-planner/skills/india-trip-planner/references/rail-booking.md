# Indian Railways: planning notes

Written from general knowledge, late 2026. Rules change: confirm each on IRCTC or a current news source before calling it certain.

## Booking window
- Advance reservation opens 60 days before the journey date, not counting the journey day (cut from 120 days in November 2024). Booking date = journey date minus 60 days. A few trains differ.
- The window counts from the date the train leaves its origin station, not the date it reaches the user's boarding station. For a train that started the day before, booking opens a day earlier. Verify on IRCTC.
- If the opening date has already passed, the booking is open now: tell the user to book today.
- Popular trains and festival dates sell out on the opening morning (08:00). Put the opening date in the booking calendar; tell the user to be logged in with payment ready. Since 2025 the first minutes of opening-day booking have been limited to Aadhaar-verified accounts; verify the current rule.
- Book the return leg on its own opening date. It is usually the harder one.

## Tatkal
Opens the day before departure: AC classes 10:00, non-AC 11:00. Aadhaar-verified IRCTC account and OTP required (2025 rules). Costs more and sells out in minutes. A last resort, not a plan.

## Waitlist
- CNF confirmed; RAC shared berth, travels; GNWL clears most often; RLWL, PQWL clear least; then "regret" or "no more booking".
- The chart is prepared hours before departure (rule changed in 2025; verify), so the final status comes late. On a hard-dated trip never rely on a long waitlist; book a second option.
- Boarding from a larger nearby station often has better availability than a small one.
- Split journeys on separate tickets are not protected if the first train runs late. Leave a long connection, or avoid them with elders.

## Classes, cheapest to most comfortable
2S (seated), SL (sleeper, non-AC), 3E (AC 3-tier economy), 3A, 2A, 1A; CC and EC on chair-car trains such as Vande Bharat and Shatabdi.

## Elders and children
- Tick "lower berth preference" for elders. A lower-berth quota exists for men 60+, women 45+ and pregnant women, but it is not guaranteed. The senior-citizen fare discount was withdrawn in 2020; verify whether it has been restored.
- Children under 5 travel free without a berth. Ages 5–11 pay full fare with a berth, or a reduced fare without one (not allowed on some premium trains). 12 and over pay full fare.

## Reading aggregator results
Aggregator fares are often shown for the train's whole route; recompute for the user's segment. "NA" or blank availability means no data, not "no seats". When sources conflict, say so and send the user to the IRCTC app.

## Sources and link formats
- IRCTC (irctc.co.in, app): booking and live availability. Authoritative.
- NTES (enquiry.indianrail.gov.in): running status and platform.
- Aggregators (ixigo, ConfirmTkt, RailYatri, erail, indiarailinfo): schedules and waitlist trends. Their availability can be stale, so cite as indicative.
- Links for the plan page, where `NNNNN` is the train number:
  - Running status: `https://www.railyatri.in/live-train-status/NNNNN`
  - Schedule: `https://erail.in/train-enquiry/NNNNN`
- Zone or Railway Board press releases: special trains and timetable changes.

## Beyond trains
- State buses: each state transport corporation has its own booking site (for example HRTC, UPSRTC, RSRTC, KSRTC, MSRTC); redBus and AbhiBus aggregate them with private operators.
- Flights: compare the nearest airport plus road against a farther, bigger airport with more flights.
- Road distances and times: Google Maps. Hill roads take far longer than the distance suggests; add margin in monsoon.
